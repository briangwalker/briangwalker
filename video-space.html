Video parameter space

A structured map of the measurable variables involved in capturing, processing, and assembling video — built to find out whether a photo, a 40‑second video, stop‑motion, and a timelapse are really different techniques, or just different regions of the same small set of numbers.

Video parameter space
Mapping — Research draft v2

Six spaces (Meta, Content, Capture, Audio, Post, Editing), two confirmed bridge variables connecting them, a third bridge under review, and a handful of values that look independent but are actually derived readouts computed from the others. Named techniques like timelapse, slow‑motion, and rack focus turn out to be labeled regions within this same space, not new dimensions.

Where it's headed

The 3D prototype above (Content space only, v7) is the first component space built out this way — camera position, heading (now with roll as a third axis), and a derived focus point, with a real second camera rendering the POV shot and computing depth of field live. The next step is extending the same pattern to the other five spaces.

Photo and stop-motion sit at opposite ends of the frame-count axis but share one thing: an undefined speed ratio. Photography breaks continuity with a single sample (N=1); stop-motion breaks it with many samples that aren't temporally regular (R undefined).

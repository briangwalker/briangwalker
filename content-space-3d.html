Prototype — v7

Content space, in 3D

An interactive Content-space explorer: drag to orbit, scroll/pinch to zoom, click a variable in the HUD (or use WASD / arrow keys) to adjust it live. The inset is a real second camera rendering an actual picture-in-picture POV shot — field of view and depth of field are computed from focal length, sensor width, and aperture, not faked.

Roll (ρ) is the third camera-heading axis alongside yaw and pitch — set it to 90° for vertical video: a rotated landscape frame, not a separate crop shape. Objects inside the computed depth-of-field range and inside the camera's actual view frustum get an amber outline, in both the orbit view and the POV shot itself.
